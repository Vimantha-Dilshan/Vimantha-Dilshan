Senior Software Engineer with **5+ years of experience** delivering full-stack, cloud-native systems at enterprise scale. I architect the invisible backbone that millions of users rely on every day carrier-grade microservices, PCI DSS-compliant payment platforms, and real-time billing infrastructure built to the highest standards of security, reliability, and performance.

My work sits at the intersection of engineering depth and business impact implementing Zero Trust architectures, tokenization, and compliance frameworks such as **ISO 27001**, **SOC 2**, and **GDPR** to protect both organizations and the users they serve. Beyond shipping code, I invest in people: mentoring engineers, driving architectural design reviews, and cultivating a culture of clean, maintainable, and secure software.
